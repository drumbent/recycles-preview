ABOUT RE-CYCLES

Our story

An Ottawa community bicycle shop built around reuse, access and shared knowledge.

Why re-Cycles exists

Put more bicycles on the road through affordable recycled bikes; make tools and repair space accessible; keep reusable bikes and parts out of landfill; and bring bicycle enthusiasts together.

How it began

Founder Lloyd Deane drew inspiration from Recycle Cycles in Kitchener-Waterloo and helped bring the community bicycle-shop idea to Ottawa.

In 2011, re-Cycles became an incorporated not-for-profit organization.

A community effort

The shop has been sustained by volunteers, mechanics, supporters and community members for many years.

The original website includes a detailed history written by founder Lloyd Deane. That archival story can be incorporated in a later revision.
